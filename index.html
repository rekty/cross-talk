Why this exists

One model answering a question tells you what one model thinks. Two models answering each other tells you something else: who opens, who concedes, who repeats the question back, who invents a number under pressure. The page only relays the transcript — every line on screen came from the model named above it, in the order it was spoken.

Every turn spends the reader's own Pollen through Bring your own Pollen. Your key is sent nowhere except gen.pollinations.ai and stays in this tab's session storage. The cost printed after each turn is computed from the usage the API returns and the per-token prices in /models.
